CheckOff Books provides a simple, done-for-you alternative to traditional accounting setups:

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to connect or link my bank accounts?

No. CheckOff Books operates from your uploaded monthly statements and does not require linking bank accounts or third-party bank feeds.

What file formats are supported for statement uploads?

Statements can be uploaded in standard PDF or CSV formats.

What is the turnaround time for monthly reports?

We provide a five-business-day turnaround for your review-ready Profit & Loss statement.

Can I receive a Balance Sheet in addition to a Profit & Loss statement?

Yes. Balance-sheet delivery is available with additional information provided by the client.

Do I need a QuickBooks subscription to use CheckOff Books?

No. CheckOff Books is a completely done-for-you service and does not require a QuickBooks subscription or software setup.
