Start the New Year With Clean Books: A January Checklist

A few small steps in January make the whole year of bookkeeping easier.

1. Close out last year first

Before you think about the new year, make sure last year is finished:

  • Download December statements for every business account once they close
  • Make sure every month of last year is categorized and reconciled
  • Get a full-year Profit & Loss to share with your tax professional

See our year-end bookkeeping checklist for the details.

2. Separate business and personal accounts

If business and personal spending still share an account or card, the new year is a natural time to separate them. Clean separation makes every month of bookkeeping simpler and clearer.

3. Review your accounts

List every account your business uses: checking, savings, credit cards and any payment platforms that deposit to your bank. Close accounts you no longer need. Fewer accounts means fewer statements to manage each month.

4. Set up a statement folder for the year

Create a folder for the new year with a subfolder for each account. Use a consistent file name, such as Bank-Account-YYYY-MM.pdf. See how to download your statements.

5. Pick a monthly routine

Choose a day each month, a few days after your statements close, to download and send them. Bookkeeping stays manageable when it happens monthly, not all at once in the spring.

6. Decide who does the bookkeeping

If you spent last year fixing bank feeds and re-categorizing transactions yourself, consider handing it off. A done-for-you service can take the monthly work off your plate.
