How to Download Your Bank and Credit-Card Statements as PDF or CSV Files

Your monthly statements are the foundation of clean books. Here is how to get them, and how to keep them organized.

Why statements matter for bookkeeping

Your bank and credit-card statements are the official record of every dollar that moved through your business accounts. With statement-based bookkeeping, they are also the only thing you need to send. No bank login, no app connection, no feed to fix.

Where to find your statements

Every bank's website looks a little different, but statements are usually in the same few places:

  • A Statements or Documents tab when you open an account
  • A Statements & Documents or eStatements link in the main menu
  • Under Account Services or Account Details

Credit-card issuers follow the same pattern. If you cannot find it, search the bank's help section for "download statement."

PDF or CSV: which should you download?

PDF statements

A PDF is the official monthly statement, the same document your bank would mail you. It shows the opening balance, every transaction and the closing balance for the period. Almost every bank offers it, and it works well for bookkeeping.

CSV files

A CSV is a spreadsheet-style export of your transactions. Many banks offer it under "Download transactions" or "Export." If you use CSV, choose the full statement period (for example, the first to the last day of the month) so nothing is left out.

Either format works. If you are unsure, download the PDF statement.

Tips for downloading cleanly

  • Download one statement per account per month. Checking, savings and each credit card each have their own statement.
  • Wait for the statement to close. A statement is final once the statement period ends. Activity downloaded mid-month will be incomplete.
  • Check the dates. Make sure the statement covers the full month you need.
  • Do not edit the file. Upload it exactly as the bank provides it.

Keep them organized

A simple naming habit saves time later. For example: BankName-Checking-1234-2026-11.pdf. Keep a folder for each year with a subfolder for each account, and you will always be able to find what you need at year-end.
