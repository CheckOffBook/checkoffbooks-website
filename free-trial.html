FAQ

Questions about the free month

Do I need a credit card to start?+

No. The trial month is free and you don't enter payment information to start it.

What happens after the free month?+

Once you have your P&L, you decide. If you want to keep going, it's $79/month for up to 3 accounts, the same price for as long as you stay. If not, you don't owe anything.

Which statement should I send?+

The most recent full-month statement for the one account you choose. If you're behind on several months, the free trial isn't the right fit. Our catch-up bookkeeping service handles that.

Do I have to link my bank?+

Never. You upload a PDF or CSV statement you download from your bank. No logins, no linking, no syncing.

Who is this a good fit for?+

Small businesses that want clean monthly numbers without running accounting software, including contractors and consultants whose CRM or job software already handles invoicing.
