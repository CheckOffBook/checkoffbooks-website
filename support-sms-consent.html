Support SMS Consent

CheckOff Books is operated by Haven Lane Advisory LLC. This page describes consent for operational SMS messages sent to authorized CheckOff Books support staff.

Who receives these messages

Only authorized CheckOff Books support staff who voluntarily provide a mobile phone number for support operations are enrolled. These messages are internal customer-care notifications and are not marketing or promotional messages.

Consent disclosure

Consent language presented to support staff:

“I agree to receive CheckOff Books SMS notifications related to client support requests at the mobile number I provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.”

A staff member's number is not activated for support SMS until the staff member has been shown this disclosure and explicitly agrees. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Message content and frequency

Messages may notify staff of new or updated client support requests and may include operational instructions for claiming, replying to, or resolving a support conversation. Message frequency varies based on client support activity.

Opt-out and help

Recipients may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for assistance. Standard message and data rates may apply.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information are used only for the support purpose described above. Mobile information and SMS consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to deliver and operate the messaging service.
