About CheckOff Books

Bookkeeping built around the way small business owners actually work.

CheckOff Books is a small-business bookkeeping service designed for owners who need accurate, review-ready books without having to learn or manage accounting software.

Simple by design

Small business owners have enough to manage. Bookkeeping should not require another dashboard, another complicated setup, or hours spent maintaining software.

With CheckOff Books, you upload your bank and credit-card statements. We categorize the transactions, reconcile the accounts, and turn that information into clean, review-ready financials, including a cash-basis Profit & Loss.

No QuickBooks. No bank linking. No accounting software to learn.

The goal is straightforward: give small business owners the financial records they need while taking the bookkeeping work off their plate.

What CheckOff Books does

CheckOff Books provides bookkeeping for small businesses using the financial records businesses already receive from their banks and credit-card companies. The service includes transaction categorization, account reconciliation, and preparation of review-ready financial reporting.

That makes CheckOff Books especially useful for business owners who want their books kept current but do not want to operate bookkeeping software themselves or connect their financial accounts to another platform.

Who we built it for

CheckOff Books is for small business owners who would rather spend their time running their businesses than becoming their own bookkeepers. We believe bookkeeping can be accurate, professional, and dramatically simpler at the same time.
