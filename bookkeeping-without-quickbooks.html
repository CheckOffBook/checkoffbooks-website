For many micro-businesses, solo practitioners, and service providers, accounting software can add recurring fees, bank-feed troubleshooting, setup complexity, and hours of transaction review.

CheckOff Books removes the software middleman. Provide your monthly bank and credit card statements, and our team delivers clean, review-ready cash-basis Profit & Loss reports.

How Our Software-Free Bookkeeping Works

  1. Upload PDF or CSV bank and credit card statements through our self-service portal.
  2. We reconcile and categorize transactions on a cash basis.
  3. Receive monthly cash-basis P&Ls for your review or to share with your tax professional.

What CheckOff Books Delivers

  • Monthly cash-basis Profit & Loss statements
  • No accounting software required
  • No direct bank linking or banking credentials
  • Reports you can provide to your CPA or tax preparer for their review
  • Balance sheets upon request when sufficient opening balances and supporting records are supplied

What We Do Not Do

We do not calculate or file tax returns; provide tax advice, tax resolution, or IRS representation; manage accounts payable; run payroll; or issue customer invoices.

Can my tax professional work without QuickBooks files?

Often, yes, but requirements vary by business and return. CheckOff Books provides organized cash-basis Profit & Loss reports that you can give your CPA or tax preparer for review along with any other records they request.
