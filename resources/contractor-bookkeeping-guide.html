Small Business Contractor Bookkeeping: Essential Expense Categories & Practices

Trade businesses can simplify monthly recordkeeping without adding more software to the job.

Core Categories to Track Consistently

  • Materials and supplies
  • Subcontractor payments
  • Tools and equipment
  • Vehicles, fuel, tolls, and parking
  • Insurance and licensing

The appropriate tax treatment can depend on the facts, so bookkeeping categories should be reviewed with your tax professional when necessary.

Why Statement-Based Bookkeeping Can Work for Trades

For contractors whose needs fit straightforward cash-basis bookkeeping, monthly bank and credit-card statements can provide an efficient foundation for categorization and reconciliation without maintaining accounting software yourself.
