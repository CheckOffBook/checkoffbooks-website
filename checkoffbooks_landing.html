FAQ

Questions people usually ask

Do I ever need to link my bank account?+

No — never. You upload a PDF or CSV statement (whatever your bank lets you download), and that's the entire connection between your bank and us. No logins, no linking, no syncing to break.

What's included in the $79 trial?+

Your first month, covering up to 3 accounts. Upload their statements and you'll get a review-ready, cash-basis Profit & Loss in 5 business days, the same as any regular month — it's a real first month of books, not a limited demo. Need to cover more than 3 accounts? Each additional statement is $10/month.

What does it cost after my trial?+

Your subscription continues at $99/month for up to 3 accounts, billed monthly — cancel anytime. Each additional statement beyond 3 is $10/month. Prefer to pay once a year? Pay for 12 months upfront and save 20% — $950.40/year instead of $1,188.

What file types do you accept?+

PDF or CSV statements only. We don't accept photos or screenshots of statements — they're unreliable to read accurately, and we'd rather get your numbers right the first time.

What does "review-ready" mean, exactly?+

It means your P&L is done, categorized, and reconciled — ready for your eyes. Occasionally we'll flag a transaction we have a question about and note it for you to weigh in on before that cycle finalizes. That's the only back-and-forth involved, and it's optional.

Will I ever need to talk to a bookkeeper?+

Only if you want to, or if we flag something on our end that genuinely needs your input — like an unclear transaction. Outside of that, everything runs on its own.

What do I actually receive each month?+

A Profit & Loss statement, by month and year-to-date — clean, simple, cash basis. No login required to view it, no extra software. Want a Balance Sheet too? Just ask — see the question below.

Is my financial data secure?+

Your statements and reports are stored in an encrypted, access-controlled portal that only you can log into — with a one-click emailed link instead of a password to remember.

Do you provide a Balance Sheet?+

On request, yes. Our standard monthly deliverable is a Profit & Loss, since that's what we can build directly from your statements. A Balance Sheet needs more — additional information from you about your assets, liabilities, equity, loans, fixed assets, and other balances. If you'd like one, just ask and we'll send a short questionnaire to gather that information; from then on, a Balance Sheet comes with your regular reports.

Is this cash basis or accrual?+

Cash basis only. We prepare your reports using the bank and credit-card statements — and any other information — you provide, and your Profit & Loss reflects the transactions in those records. No accrual adjustments or unbilled revenue. If your business needs full accrual-basis financials, this service isn't the right fit yet.
